# Clean-Room Rules

1. Do not copy production customer, address, sales, employee, vendor, network, or credential data.
2. Do not reuse company logos, names, private templates, secrets, or environment values.
3. Build generic business capabilities from documented requirements and observed behavior, not company datasets.
4. Use synthetic seed data only.
5. External systems are represented by configurable integrations; no employer CRM name belongs in the core schema.
6. Team names, dispositions, offers, lifecycle stages, schedules, and invoice prefixes are tenant configuration.
